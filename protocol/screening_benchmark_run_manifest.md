The production GPT-4o-mini screen was run on 15 May 2026; the GPT-5.4-mini and Mistral-small-2603 benchmark runs were completed on 20 May 2026; final gold reconciliation and evaluation were completed on 22 May 2026. Requested and API-returned model identifiers are shown above where retained by the run artifacts.

Normalized resource use per 100 records was: GPT-4o-mini, USD 0.064 and 355.0 s; GPT-5.4-mini, USD 0.356 and 204.8 s; Mistral-small-2603, USD 0.067 and 205.2 s; and TF-IDF plus logistic regression, USD 0 and 0.5 s. GPT-4o-mini values derive from the 1,530-record production run; GPT-5.4-mini and Mistral values derive from the 794-record benchmark runs; the baseline runtime derives from its training summary and out-of-fold prediction generation.

The benchmark was intentionally enriched for difficult and ambiguous records: 644 hard/ambiguous records plus 150 confident-include quality-control records. It evaluates screening safety for this review rather than general model performance. A false exclusion is a model prediction of Exclude for a human Include_Core, Include_Supporting, or Maybe record; a high-risk false exclusion is restricted to human Include_Core or Include_Supporting. Public row-level predictions are supplied in `screening_benchmark_gold_and_predictions.csv`; titles, abstracts, rationales, and internal audit notes are deliberately excluded.
